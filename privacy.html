Privacy

Short and specific: what is collected, why, and where it is kept.

The E-Sys MAX program

Everything you do with your car stays on your PC: vehicle orders, coding files, backups, fault lists and reports are saved in your own folders and never uploaded.

For the licence, the launcher sends the licence server:

  • your licence text and the PC ID (a code made from the PC's motherboard);
  • short codes made from the PC's parts (motherboard, processor, memory, Windows, system disk). They are one-way hashes: the serial numbers themselves are never sent;
  • when you ask for a licence or a trial: the PC's model, processor, memory size, Windows version and computer name, so a licence can be matched to the right PC;
  • the E-Sys MAX version.

This happens when the launcher starts with internet (the weekly licence check), and when you start a trial or ask for a licence.

Buying

Card payments are handled by Stripe. We receive your name, e-mail address, the plan and the PC ID; your card details go to Stripe only. In Telegram we see your Telegram name and the messages you send to @EsysMaxbot.

Where it is kept

Licence records (licences, PC IDs, the hardware codes above, order e-mail addresses, Telegram names) are kept in a private database that only the licence server can read, and are used only to issue, check, move and support licences. We do not sell or share them, and we do not use advertising trackers on this website.

Your requests

To see or delete what is kept about you, write to @EsysMaxbot. Deleting your records ends the licence on the next check.
