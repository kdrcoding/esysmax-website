Private by design, made in the USA

No account. No data grab.

E-Sys MAX keeps only what a licence needs. Everything about your car stays on your PC.

Stays on your PC

Never uploaded by E-Sys MAX.

  • Vehicle orders (FA), SVT and I-steps
  • Every coding file (NCD) and coding change
  • Full backups, fault lists and car reports
  • Your VINs (except inside a problem report you choose to send)
  • Settings you search, and names you type (unless you send them)

What we keep

Only for your licence.

  • Your licence and its PC ID (a code made from the motherboard)
  • One-way codes made from the PC's parts, never the serial numbers
  • PC model, processor, memory, Windows version and computer name
  • Buying by card: name, e-mail and country from Stripe, for your receipt
  • Free trial: a one-way code of the first car's VIN, never the VIN itself

Only when you choose

Off until you press Send or turn it on.

  • Problem report: logs, sent only when you press Send
  • Help improve names: you see the file first; never VINs or coding values
  • Anonymous usage counts: off unless you turn them on; never a VIN
  • Telegram: only if you write to @EsysMaxbot
