E-Sys: the most control

E-Sys is BMW's engineering tool. It reads every module and lets you change any setting, which is why serious coders use it. The trade-off is that it is made for engineers: settings are in German shorthand, most newer ones show only as a dot, and one wrong click can reset a module.

You need a Windows laptop, E-Sys, PSdZData for your car, an ENET cable or ICOM, and a battery charger.

Phone apps: quick and simple

Phone coding apps connect through an OBD adapter and offer a list of popular changes with switches. They are great for the common things and need no laptop. You can only change what the app offers for your car, and adapters and subscriptions add up over several cars.

Dealer tools: diagnosis first

Dealer diagnosis software finds faults, runs service functions and programs modules. It codes from the vehicle order, not single settings, so it is the right tool for repairs and retrofits rather than personal changes.

Which one should I pick?

  • A few popular changes on one car, no laptop: a phone app.
  • Full control, many cars or anything a phone app does not list: E-Sys, ideally with E-Sys MAX so you can read what you change.
  • Repairs, programming and fault finding as a workshop: a dealer tool, often alongside E-Sys.

Questions

Is E-Sys free?

E-Sys is BMW software and is not sold by us. E-Sys MAX works with the E-Sys already on your PC and does not include it.

Can I use a Bluetooth adapter with E-Sys?

No. E-Sys needs a wired Ethernet connection: an ENET cable (Ethernet to OBD) or an ICOM.

Do I need a Mac or Windows?

E-Sys and E-Sys MAX run on Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. See Compatibility.
