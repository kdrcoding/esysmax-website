Licence

Your licence

Activate it, get it back after a reinstall, or move a lifetime licence to a new PC.

Activate

  1. Open the E-Sys MAX launcher, Help > Licence.
  2. Paste the licence (the text that starts with EMAX1.) into box 1.
  3. Press Activate. The launcher shows what the licence is and until when.

Got your licence in Telegram? You don't need to paste anything: it arrives by itself when E-Sys MAX starts, or press Refresh (top right) or Help > Licence > Check for my licence.

Find my licence

Bought by card? Your e-mail and PC ID give your licence back, for example after a Windows reinstall.

In the E-Sys MAX launcher: Help > Licence > Copy this PC's ID.

Bought in Telegram? Ask the bot

New PC

A licence is for one PC. A lifetime licence can be moved to a new PC one time, by its owner, for example when the old PC broke or was replaced: on the new PC, open Help > Licence > Get a licence on Telegram and choose Move my licence to this PC. Once approved you get the licence for the new PC in Telegram; the old PC stops at its next licence check.

No Telegram? Use the contact form, topic Move my lifetime licence to a new PC, with your licence ID, the e-mail of your order and the reason for the move.

A 1-month or 1-year licence stays on the PC it was bought for and cannot be moved to another PC. 1-year licences bought before 3 October 2026 can still be moved.

Your licence is tied to the motherboard: a Windows reinstall, a new SSD or more RAM on the same PC do not need a move, whatever the plan.

The weekly check

When the launcher starts with internet, it confirms the licence with the licence server in a second. After that E-Sys MAX works offline for up to 7 days. After 7 days without a check it asks for the internet once.

What is sent

The licence, the PC ID, short codes made from the PC's hardware (never the serial numbers themselves) and the E-Sys MAX version. Nothing about your car. See privacy.
