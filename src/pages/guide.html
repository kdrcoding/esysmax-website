Quick Start Guide

From download to your first coding change

Everything a new user needs, step by step. Download this guide as a PDF. Version 1.2 · esysmax.com

1. What you need

  • A Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit) with internet at least once a week.
  • BMW E-Sys with PSdZData, installed on that PC (not included with E-Sys MAX).
  • An ENET cable to the car, and a battery charger connected to the car while you code.

2. Install

  1. Download the installer from esysmax.com/download and run it. The installer shows the Terms; accept them to continue.
  2. Open E-Sys MAX from the Start menu. The first time, read the short "Terms and safety" summary and accept it.
  3. The launcher lists the E-Sys versions it found on your PC.

3. Try it free for 3 days

  1. In the launcher open Help > Licence and press Start the free 3-day trial. No card needed.
  2. The launcher shows an orange FREE TRIAL badge with the days left.
  3. The trial works on one car: the first car you connect becomes the trial car.

4. Buy a licence (2 minutes)

  1. In the launcher open Help > Licence and press Buy by card. Your browser opens esysmax.com/buy with your PC ID already filled in.
  2. Choose 1 month ($39), 1 year ($59) or Lifetime ($99), tick the three boxes and press Pay by card. Payment is handled securely by Stripe. Every licence is a one-time payment; nothing renews by itself.
  3. Right after paying, the page shows your licence. Press Copy licence.
  4. Back in the launcher: Help > Licence, paste it, press Activate. Done.

Prefer Telegram? Press Get a licence on Telegram instead: @EsysMaxbot takes your order there. A licence you get in Telegram arrives by itself: just restart E-Sys MAX or press Refresh (top right). Nothing to copy.

5. Your first session

  1. Connect the charger and the ENET cable. Press play on your E-Sys version in the launcher: E-Sys opens with E-Sys MAX inside.
  2. Connect to the car in E-Sys, then make a full backup first: E-Sys MAX > Full backup of the car.
  3. Read the coding data and open a module in the FDL-Editor. The E-Sys MAX panel shows every setting in English and What can I code? lists ready-made changes for your car.
  4. Check every change before you code it. When in doubt, don't.

6. Useful tools

  • Search the car: find any setting in every module by name.
  • Coding history: see exactly what changed, and open an older version to put it back.
  • Fault scan: read and clear the fault memory of every module.
  • Car report (PDF): model, options, modules, changes and faults in one document.

7. Your licence

  • One licence works on one PC. A Windows reinstall, new SSD or more RAM keep it.
  • E-Sys MAX checks the licence online at least every 7 days and works offline in between.
  • New laptop? A lifetime licence moves with you: in the launcher on the new PC, Help > Licence > Get a licence on Telegram > Move my licence to this PC. A 1-month or 1-year licence stays on the PC it was bought for.
  • Lost your licence text after a reinstall? Use esysmax.com/licence with your order e-mail and PC ID.

8. Safety and legal

Vehicle coding is at your own risk: it can damage control units, change safety systems and make a car illegal to drive. Back up first, keep a charger connected, and only code changes that are legal where the car is used. E-Sys MAX is not affiliated with BMW AG.
