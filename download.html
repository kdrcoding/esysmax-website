Install and start the trial

  1. Run the installer. Windows may ask once whether to run it: choose More info > Run anyway.
  2. Open E-Sys MAX from the Start menu. It finds your E-Sys installations by itself.
  3. Go to Help > Licence and press Start the free 3-day trial. One trial per PC, no card. The launcher shows FREE TRIAL and the days left.
  4. Press play on your E-Sys version: E-Sys opens with E-Sys MAX in its menu and toolbar.
  5. Connect your car. The trial works on this one car. Before you code anything: E-Sys MAX > Full backup of the car.

You need

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • BMW E-Sys installed, with PSdZData (not included)
  • An ENET cable to the car
  • A battery charger on the car while coding
  • Internet at least once a week, for the licence check

Updates

The launcher tells you when a new version is out and installs it with one click. Every update is signed: a changed or broken download is refused.
