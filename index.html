FAQ

Questions

What do I need besides E-Sys MAX?

Four things: BMW E-Sys (any version from 3.x to 25.x works), PSdZData for it (the Lite pack is enough for coding; the Full pack is needed only for flashing), an ENET cable from your laptop to the car's OBD port (no Ethernet port? a USB Ethernet adapter works), and a battery charger on the car while you code. E-Sys and PSdZData are BMW software: they are not included and not supplied by E-Sys MAX, so use the E-Sys and PSdZData you already have. The launcher finds them by itself and shows a setup check on Home: E-Sys, PSdZData and the ENET cable, each green when it is ready, with a hint when something is missing.

Is E-Sys included?

No. E-Sys MAX adds to BMW's E-Sys and works with the E-Sys, PSdZData and ENET cable you already use. It finds your installed E-Sys versions by itself.

Which cars does it work with?

The BMW models E-Sys codes: the F series and the G series. Try it on your car for free first. Setting names are most complete for the F series; G-series names grow with every update.

Can it change my car without me knowing?

No. Reading, searching, history and reports never write to the car. A write only happens when you press Yes in E-Sys or in E-Sys MAX, and No is always the default.

What is in the free trial?

Every feature for 3 days, on one car: the first car you connect with E-Sys MAX becomes the trial's car. E-Sys itself keeps working on every car; a licence unlocks E-Sys MAX on all of them. One free trial per PC and per car.

Does it need the internet?

Coding does not. The licence is checked online when the launcher starts, and E-Sys MAX keeps working offline for up to 7 days after the last check. Connect once a week and you never notice it.

I reinstall Windows or change the SSD. Do I lose the licence?

No. Your licence is tied to the motherboard, not to Windows: a Windows reinstall, a new SSD or more RAM keep it, whatever the plan. That is the same PC, not a move. If your licence text is gone after a reinstall, get it again on the licence page or from @EsysMaxbot.

I bought a new laptop. Can I move my licence?

With a lifetime licence, yes, one time: open Help > Licence > Get a licence on Telegram on the new laptop and choose "Move my licence to this PC" (or use the contact form with your licence ID, the e-mail of your order and the reason). Once approved, the licence works on the new laptop and the old one stops. A 1-month or 1-year licence stays on the PC it was bought for and cannot be moved. 1-year licences bought before 3 October 2026 can still be moved.

Can I use one licence on two PCs?

No, a licence is for one PC at a time. For a second PC, buy a second licence. A lifetime licence can be moved to a new PC one time, when you change computers; a 1-month or 1-year licence stays on the PC it was bought for.

Does a licence renew by itself?

No. Every licence is a one-time payment and nothing is charged again: 1 month lasts 30 days, 1 year lasts 365 days, lifetime never ends. To keep going after a 1-month or 1-year licence, buy again for the same PC. If the current licence is still running, the new period starts when it ends, so you lose no days.

Which plan should I choose?

For one job on one car, 1 month is enough. For more than that, 1 year costs $59, about 16¢ a day and less than 2 months of the monthly plan (12 months of the monthly plan cost $468). If you will still be coding after that, lifetime ($99) costs less than 2 years of the 1-year plan ($118) and less than 3 months of the monthly plan ($117). It never needs renewing, gets every future update and priority support, and is the only plan you can move to a new PC (one time). Not sure about lifetime yet? Start with 1 year: while it runs, the upgrade to lifetime costs only $40.

Can I upgrade from 1 year to lifetime?

Yes, at any time while your 1-year licence is running: your $59 counts, so lifetime costs only $40 (the $99 lifetime price less the $59 you paid). Buy lifetime for the same PC on the buy page or in @EsysMaxbot; the payment page shows the lower price. The lifetime licence starts at once and replaces the 1-year one on that PC. A 1-month licence does not count toward the upgrade.

Do you sell to customers in the USA?

Yes. E-Sys MAX is made by KDR Coding, a business in Los Angeles, California. Prices are in US dollars, payment is by card through Stripe, and the licence is delivered instantly, so there is nothing to ship. Sales tax may apply where required.
