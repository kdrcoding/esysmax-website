The rules for using E-Sys MAX.

The licence

  • A licence lets one person use E-Sys MAX on one PC, for the time bought (1 year or lifetime). A trial lasts 3 days, once per PC.
  • The licence is checked online when the launcher starts; E-Sys MAX works offline for up to 7 days after the last check.
  • A licence can be moved to another PC on request. The PC it was moved from stops working with it.
  • Sharing, reselling or getting around the licence check ends the licence.
  • "Lifetime" means for as long as E-Sys MAX is offered, including its updates.

Coding your car

  • E-Sys MAX adds to BMW's E-Sys; it does not include E-Sys or PSdZData. You need your own.
  • Coding changes how a car behaves and can break things or affect legal requirements. You decide every change and you are responsible for it. Back up before coding, keep a charger on the car, and code only what you understand and what is legal where you drive.
  • Setting names and ready-made changes are help, not a promise: check them before you code.
  • E-Sys MAX is provided as it is. As far as the law allows, we are not liable for damage to cars, control units or data that results from coding.
