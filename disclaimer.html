Vehicle Coding Disclaimer

Version 2026-10-02. Please read this before you code any vehicle.

E-Sys MAX is a tool for experienced users. You use it entirely at your own risk. E-Sys MAX helps you read and understand coding; it does not decide what is safe, legal or right for a vehicle. You do.

The risks

  • Coding can damage control units, leave a vehicle with faults or unable to start, or require a dealer to repair it.
  • Coding can switch off, change or weaken safety, driver assistance, lighting, warning, emission and other systems.
  • A coded vehicle may become unroadworthy, fail inspection, or be illegal to drive on public roads where you are.
  • Coding may affect the manufacturer's warranty, insurance cover, leasing contracts and resale value.
  • Setting names, descriptions, ready-made changes ("cheats"), reports and fault information can be incomplete or wrong, and can differ between models, versions and countries.

What you must do

  • Make a full backup before you change anything, and keep it.
  • Keep a suitable battery charger connected; never code with a weak battery.
  • Never code a vehicle while it is being driven or is not safely parked.
  • Check every change yourself before writing it; when in doubt, do not write it.
  • Make sure every change is legal where the vehicle is registered and used.
  • Code only vehicles you own or have the owner's permission to code, and tell the owner what you changed.
